Relocation FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes. Live.City is paid by partner companies for qualified introductions. You pay nothing to us, and you only pay a provider if you choose to hire them.

Who will contact me?

Up to three partners relevant to the services you ticked — for example a mover and a rental agent. We don't sell your details to list brokers.

Can I get help for a city that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose "Other / not listed" and add the city in the notes. We cover moves worldwide.

How do I research my city before moving?

Use each city's live dashboard, our comparison tool and the 90-day relocation checklist.
